# Julian Vance Blackwood

I am building a multidisciplinary technical foundation across cybersecurity, intelligent systems, defense technology, aerospace, electronics, and computational finance.

My interests are centered on systems where software, hardware, data, and real-world constraints intersect. I am particularly interested in understanding how complex systems are designed, secured, analyzed, and operated under demanding conditions.

Network security, operating systems, security engineering, vulnerability research, digital forensics, reverse engineering, and malware analysis.

**Artificial Intelligence & Intelligence Systems**
Machine learning, data analysis, information systems, decision-support systems, automation, and the application of computation to complex analytical problems.

**Defense & Autonomous Systems**
Autonomous platforms, sensing, command-and-control architectures, resilient communications, embedded computing, and mission-critical systems.

**Aerospace & Space Systems**
Flight systems, spacecraft and launch technologies, avionics, telemetry, guidance and navigation, communications, and the software infrastructure behind aerospace operations.

**Electronics & Embedded Systems**
Digital electronics, computer architecture, microcontrollers, embedded software, sensors, communications, and hardware-software integration.

Financial markets, quantitative methods, cryptography, distributed systems, blockchain infrastructure, risk, and the engineering behind modern financial platforms.

**Behavioral & Analytical Sciences**
Decision-making, human behavior, cognitive bias, structured analysis, and the interaction between people, information, and technology.

## Current Direction

I am developing these disciplines from first principles with an emphasis on fundamentals, experimentation, and implementation.

My work here will document that process through code, technical notes, laboratories, experiments, system designs, security research, and increasingly complex engineering projects.

The objective is not to collect technologies or credentials.

It is to develop the ability to understand difficult systems, break complex problems into tractable components, build reliable solutions, and continuously improve the depth of my technical judgment.

## Engineering Principles

I value systems that are understandable, measurable, secure, and built with purpose.

I prefer fundamentals over abstraction without understanding.

I believe technical claims should be supported by working implementations, reproducible experiments, or clear analysis.

I treat failure as information: test assumptions, identify the cause, document what changed, and iterate.

I am interested in difficult problems precisely because they expose the limits of what I know.
