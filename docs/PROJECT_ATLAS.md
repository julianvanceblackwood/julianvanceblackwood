## What makes a review useful

A reproducible observation is more useful than a broad assessment. State the relevant commit, execution environment, inputs or fixtures, expected output, observed output and any limiting assumptions. For a claim about a planned feature, distinguish an architectural proposal from an implemented result.

- **Engineering review:** minimal counterexample, failing test, reproducible performance measurement or normative protocol discrepancy.
- **Research review:** falsifiable hypothesis, defined baseline, controlled experiment, limitation or alternative explanation.
- **Security report:** use the relevant repository's SECURITY.md instructions, not a public issue.
- **Private collaboration inquiry:** explain the concrete research boundary, proposed contribution and conditions; contact [julianvanceblackwood@gmail.com](mailto:julianvanceblackwood@gmail.com).
